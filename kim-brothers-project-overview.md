# Project Overview

## What this is

The public marketing website for **Kim Brothers Ent.**, a Malaysia-based operation run by Lam Kim Sim (Chris), CEO. Based in Putra Heights, Selangor.

## What Kim Brothers does

Four core service lines:

1. **Automation & AI agents** — WhatsApp sales bots, workflow automation, custom agent builds for SMEs
2. **Web development** — production-grade Next.js sites for clients
3. **Scraping & data pipelines** — custom crawlers, data processing, automated reporting
4. **SaaS products** — EasyStaff (J&T payroll), SmartChiro (chiropractic), BizzFlow (fibre ops)

Supporting line:

5. **Digital marketing** — Meta & Google Ads, creative, lead attribution

## Primary audience

Malaysian SME owner-operators who have a business problem tech can solve but don't have an in-house dev team.

## What this site has to do

1. Communicate the four service lines clearly in plain language
2. Show proof of work through case studies (7 projects, fixed order)
3. Drive organic Google traffic — current site is client-rendered and invisible to crawlers, which is the main reason for the rebuild
4. Convert visitors to WhatsApp conversations or contact form submissions

## Pages

- `/` — Home (hero, services, how-it-works, featured work, CTA)
- `/services` — Full service breakdown
- `/work` — Case study index
- `/work/[slug]` — Individual case studies (7 total, fixed order)
- `/about` — Founder-led short page
- `/blog` — MDX-based long-form content for SEO
- `/blog/[slug]` — Individual posts
- `/contact` — WhatsApp CTA + email + contact form
- `/404` — Custom on-brand error page
